macOS asks whether OpenCharm may find devices on your local network: choose Allow, so the charm can find this Mac by itself.

  2. A page opens (or go to 192.168.4.1). Pick your Wi-Fi, 2.4 GHz, and type its password.
  3. Leave everything else as it is: the charm finds this computer by itself.
