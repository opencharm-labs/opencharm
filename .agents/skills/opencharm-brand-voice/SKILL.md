# OpenCharm brand voice

## Voice

- Plain, short sentences. Name things by what people recognise ("the key", "the face", "your agent").
- The first time charmd appears in a document: "charmd (the charm daemon)".
- Mark estimates ("about", "estimate") and anything unconfirmed ("unverified"). Never state a price or spec without a source and a date.

## Naming

- "OpenCharm" (one word, capital O and C); "charm" is fine.
- Never use "Muse" in names, handles, topics, paths or visuals. Comparisons with Meta's product live in one comparison section only. Not affiliated with Meta, Nous Research, OpenClaw, Waveshare or Espressif.

## Truths that every surface keeps

- No warranty: open source, provided as is; you build and use it at your own risk (full text in README).
- A serious project, never called a "hobby" project (maintainer, 2 October 2026).
- We sell and ship nothing: no waitlist, no pre-orders, no kits. Copy points people to building their own.
- The charm holds no API keys; the agent runs on your machine.

## Look (for pages and images)

Full visual identity (tokens, components, do's and don'ts): `DESIGN.md` at the repo root. In short:

White and black like a technical drawing: off-white `#F6F6F4` ground, faint grid, black ink, Geist / Geist Mono, pill buttons. Colour only on the device. Tokens: `packages/design/tokens.json`. The app icon: white shell, black squircle screen, `^ ^`; generated by `brand/build_icon.py`, never edit the PNGs.
