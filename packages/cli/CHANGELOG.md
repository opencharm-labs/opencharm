# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-03)

The first release of `opencharm`, published from CI with npm provenance.

- `opencharm init`: your charm's workspace from the starter (default name Momo), fitted to your machine and agent.
- `opencharm serve`: charmd, the charm daemon: pairing, PIN, lock, voice turns, and your agent over ACP (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, goose, Hermes, OpenClaw) or OpenAI-compatible adapters.
- `opencharm sim`: the charm in your browser (the firmware core in WebAssembly).
- `opencharm pair`, `lock`, `unlock`, `revoke`, `status`; `opencharm mcp`, the charm's tools for your agent.
- `opencharm faces`, `face`, `states`, `hardware`.
