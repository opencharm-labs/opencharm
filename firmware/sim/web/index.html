HOW TO USE

Hold Space (or the key on the right side) to talk. A short press stops speech or dismisses a line. Tap the face. Type the PIN on the screen.

Orange means it needs you. On a question, hold for yes, press for no.

Mac notch shows the desktop charm: its eyes beside the notch, a panel that opens below it. Type the PIN on the keyboard.
