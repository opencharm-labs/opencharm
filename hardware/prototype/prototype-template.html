THE RULE

Grow the screen. Keep the corner.

Offset the glass outline by 1.75 mm and its corner radius grows by the same 1.75 mm, so the frame is the same width all the way round.

GLASS

43.30 × 43.30 mm, R 4.70, from Waveshare’s drawing (hardware/reference).

ACTIVE AREA

38.99 × 38.99 mm, 480 × 480 px.

WINDOW

41.70 mm. A 0.6 mm lip with a 45° bevel holds the glass and hides 0.8 mm of its black border.

WALL

1.60 mm (four 0.4 mm perimeters) plus 0.15 mm clearance around the glass.

SHELL

46.80 mm, R 6.45, 22.0 mm deep. Waveshare’s own case is 46 × 46 × 22.5.

THE STRAP

No hole. It hangs from the seam.

A phone-charm cord loops round a Ø1.8 mm pin printed inside the back cover and leaves through a 2.6 × 2.2 mm notch at the top centre, so it hangs upright, face forward.

WHY THE TOP

Hung from the centre it stays upright. From a corner it would hang tilted.

THE PIN

Printed as a short bridge across a 3 mm pocket in the back cover, 1.2 mm of room for the cord. No metal, no glue.

SWAPPING

Pop the back, slip the loop off the pin, put a new one on.

ON THE DESK

Two 0.6 mm rails on the bottom so it stands and the speaker slots breathe.

ONE KEY

One key you can find without looking.

The board has three buttons on its right edge (PWR, BOOT, GPIO18). One gets a real key in a contrast colour; the other two hide behind pinholes.

RIGHT

Key: Ø5.3 mm cap, 0.8 mm proud, captive flange, on the middle button. Pinholes 10 mm above and below, pressed with a SIM tool. Which button is GPIO18 must be checked on the board.

LEFT

USB-C, 11.2 × 5.8 mm opening, centred 8.6 mm behind the front.

TOP

Strap notch at the centre, mic pinhole 10 mm right of centre. The microSD slot is covered.

BOTTOM

Five speaker slots between two desk rails, second mic pinhole 8 mm right of centre.

BACK

R 2.4 roundover and a 22 mm square name plate, 0.4 mm deep, for a sticker or paint pen.

01

Drop in the key

From inside the shell, flange against the wall.

02

Seat the board

Glass first, face down, into the pocket. Side buttons line up with the key and pinholes.

03

Plug the battery

Into the MX1.25 socket, lying behind the board.

04

Loop the strap

Around the pin in the back cover, cord out through the gap in its plug.

05

Press the back on

Friction fit, 0.1 mm per side. Line the cord up with the notch.

06

Flash and pair

USB-C, flash the firmware, pair with your agent.

BEFORE THE FIRST PRINT

What still needs a board on the bench.

?

Stack depth. The inside is 17.4 mm behind the lip and the back is a 4 mm plate (Waveshare’s own case is 22.5 mm overall). Thin the plate first, then add depth in gen.py.

?

Which side button is GPIO18. If it isn’t the middle one, move the key.

?

Key stem length (1.2 mm to start). Trim or lengthen to meet the switch.

?

What holds the board from behind. v0.1 relies on the battery and the back plug.

?

Back cover friction fit. Printers vary; FIT in gen.py.

?

Mic pinhole positions are guesses; the speaker slots follow the drawing’s grille. Check on the board.

?

Whether a speaker comes in the box. Strong evidence says yes; not confirmed by a packing list.

?

Battery capacity. Waveshare doesn’t publish the mAh.
